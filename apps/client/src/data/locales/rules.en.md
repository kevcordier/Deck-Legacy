# Kingdom Legacy — Rules

## Goal

Develop your kingdom by upgrading your cards, round after round. You cannot lose: your score is the total **Glory** ({{glory}}) of your kingdom at the end of the game.

---

## Golden Rules

1. **A card only changes side through a rule.** The app turns cards for you when an upgrade or an effect says so.
2. **You may look at every side of the cards in play and in your discard pile**, but not at the cards you have not discovered yet.
3. **A card that changes side is discarded immediately**, whether it was upgraded or turned by an effect.
4. **Upgrading a card ends your turn.**
5. **A resource sticker cannot be added to a card that already produces 9 or more resources.**

---

## A New Kingdom

Each game is a **kingdom**. On the home page, tap **New kingdom**, name it and choose whether to play the tutorial first. Your kingdom starts with cards **1–10**, shuffled into a draw deck; the other cards are waiting to be discovered.

The tutorial guides you through a first round; at its end, you can keep that game or start a new one. Your kingdoms are listed on the home page with their total score, the base game score and the score after each expansion. You can keep **one kingdom** without an account and up to **10** with an account.

---

## Rounds

A **round** is a series of turns that ends when your draw deck is empty.

- At the start of each round (from round 2), the next **2 cards** in number order are **discovered** and join your deck. If the first of them is a **parchment card**, its instructions apply immediately, then it is destroyed.
- The round preview shows the cards you just discovered; tap **▶︎ Start round** to shuffle all your cards into a new deck and play the first turn.
- The game ends after the round in which you discover card **#70**. Cards above #70 can only be discovered through card effects.

---

## Turn

Each turn starts by playing the top **4 cards** of your deck onto the board (all of them if fewer remain). The top card of your deck is always visible.

Then take actions in any order until your turn ends:

| Action         | How                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Produce**    | Tap a card's production: you gain its resources and the card is discarded. A card without production cannot produce. |
| **Use effect** | Tap an effect of a card and pay its cost, if any (see _Effects_).                                                    |
| **Upgrade**    | Tap an upgrade of a card and pay its cost: the card changes side and **your turn ends**.                             |
| **Progress**   | Play **2 more cards** from your deck. You can progress several times.                                                |
| **End turn**   | End your turn without upgrading.                                                                                     |

A card discarded by another effect does not give its production.

### Resources

Resources are gained by producing and by some effects, and spent to pay costs. **All your resources are lost as soon as a new card enters play and at the end of the turn**: gain them only when you can spend them. The app warns you before you progress with unspent resources.

|           | Resource  |            | Resource   |
| --------- | --------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| {{gold}}  | **Gold**  | {{weapon}} | **Weapon** |
| {{wood}}  | **Wood**  | {{goods}}  | **Goods**  |
| {{stone}} | **Stone** | {{iron}}   | **Iron**   |

A slash (/) between resources means "or": you choose one of them.

**New resources:** some expansions add new resources. An effect that gives "any 1 resource" includes them, and when you add "any 1 resource sticker", it may be one of them, even if the effect names stickers 1–6 (the resources of the base game).

### End of Turn and End of Round

- **End of turn:** _End of turn_ effects are performed, then every card in play is discarded, except the cards that **stay in play**. A new turn starts if your deck still has cards; otherwise the round ends.
- **End of round:** every card is discarded, even the cards that stay in play, and _End of round_ effects are performed. Then a new round starts.

## Effects

Each effect starts with an icon telling how it is used:

| Icon                | Effect                  | Use                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| {{passif}}          | **Passive**             | Always applies, or may be used repeatedly without discarding the card.                   |
| {{action}}          | **Activated**           | Discard the card to carry out the effect.                                                |
| {{time}}            | **Time**                | Discard the card (unless it is permanent) and **end your turn** to carry out the effect. |
| {{destroy}}         | **Destroy**             | Destroy the card to carry out the effect.                                                |
| {{optionalTrigger}} | **Triggered, optional** | May be used when its event happens (e.g. _End of turn_). It does not discard the card.   |
| {{trigger}}         | **Triggered, forced**   | Same, but it must be performed.                                                          |

Some effects can be used **only once**: once used, they are no longer offered.

### Pending Effects

When several effects happen at the same time, they wait in the **pending effects** panel: you choose the order in which to resolve them, and you must resolve them all, except optional ones, which you may **skip**. Cards played together (4 at the start of a turn, 2 when progressing) are all in play when their _when played_ effects are resolved.

### Effect Scope

Unless a card says otherwise, an effect only targets **cards in play**, not permanent cards. Your **kingdom** means every card in your deck, in play, in your discard pile and your permanent cards, but not blocked cards.

## Card Mechanics

### Upgrades and Sides

Most cards have several **stages**. The cost of each upgrade is shown on the card; the app turns the card to its new stage. Some effects turn a card ({{upgrade}}) without being an upgrade: this does **not** end your turn, but the card is still discarded (golden rule 3).

- **Choosing a side:** when you discover some cards, you choose which side they start on.
- **Resetting** a card turns it back to its starting stage.

### Tracks

Some cards have a row of **boxes** to mark. Marking a box may cost resources and gives what is shown in it; the card says what completing the track does. Boxes are marked in any order, unless the card says from left to right.

### Staying in Play and Permanent Cards

- A card that **stays in play** is not discarded at the end of the turn. It is still discarded when it produces, uses an effect that discards it, or at the end of the round.
- **Permanent** cards are never shuffled into your deck and cannot be discarded. Their effects always apply, but they are **not in play**.

### Blocking and Destroying

- A **blocked** card is set beneath the card that blocks it and counts as absent. If the blocking card is discarded during your turn, the blocked card comes back into play (it is not _played_); at the end of the turn or round, both are discarded.
- A **destroyed** card ({{destroy}}) leaves your kingdom for good.

### Discovering and Your Deck

- A card discovered by an effect goes to your **discard pile**. If an effect lets you discover one of several cards, you see them all before choosing.
- Some effects put cards on top of or under your deck; they cannot be used when your deck is empty. An empty deck always ends the round after the turn.

### Stickers

Some effects add **stickers** to a card: production, Glory or staying in play. Stickers come from your **sticker stock**: if none is left, the effect is ignored. **Boosting** a card means adding a sticker of a resource it already produces, so it produces 1 more.

### Equipment

Some cards have a third, red banner starting with **"Equip"** followed by a keyword: they are **equipment**, meant to be attached to a card with that keyword.

- **Not equipped**, an equipment has no production and no effects, but it still counts as a card in play with its keywords and Glory, and it can be upgraded like any other card.
- **Equipping** is an action: attach the equipment to a card in play with the required keyword. It cannot be done in reaction to another effect, such as a _when played_ effect.
- **Once equipped**, the equipment no longer counts as a card on its own: its production, Glory, keywords and effects count as part of the card holding it. For example, a person worth 3 {{glory}} holding an equipment worth 5 {{glory}} counts as worth 8 {{glory}}; a building producing 1 {{gold}} holding an equipment producing 1 {{wood}} produces both.
- If the holder is blocked or destroyed, its equipment is too. If the holder changes side, is discarded or leaves play in any other way, its equipment is discarded. An effect that crosses out the production of the holder also crosses out the production of its equipment.
- An equipped card cannot be upgraded, unequipped or equipped to another card.

### Tabbed Cards

Some effects **tab** a card with a sticker: a tabbed card stays marked and can be seen wherever it is in your deck.

## Enemies

Red cards are **negative**, usually _Enemy_ or _Event_ cards; every other card is **friendly**. Negative cards cannot be purged normally.

- **Defeating:** most enemies say how to defeat them: they are destroyed, turned, or have a box marked. An enemy without a defeat instruction cannot be defeated. Some cards react **when you defeat an enemy** (for example by letting you upgrade them): you must already have the resources they ask for.
- Some enemies **restrict your resources** while they are in play: you may only gain {{weapon}}, or you may use {{weapon}} only to upgrade cards (not for effects, tracks or {{time}} effects).
- **Prisoners** are defeated enemies. They are negative too, but after selecting the cards of a purge you may add up to 2 Prisoners to it.

## Expansions

Once you finish the base game, you can continue your kingdom with **expansions**, one at a time: each one adds rounds and often new cards and concepts. Each kingdom may play each expansion only once. Mini expansions add 4 rounds without discovering new cards each round; larger expansions add rounds and many new cards.

### Purge

Before each expansion, you **purge** your kingdom: purged cards are destroyed, but their Glory is kept in your score.

- **Purge N:** your deck is shuffled and split into batches of N cards; in each batch, choose **1 friendly card** to purge. When fewer than N cards remain, the selection is complete.
- **Purge a permanent card:** choose any friendly permanent card.
- Cards that cannot be destroyed or purged are never offered.
- **Focus:** some expansions ask for a purge with a focus, for example "focus 2 {{wood}}": in each batch, you must choose a card that produces {{wood}} if the batch has one, until you have purged 2 such cards. Then the purge continues as normal.
- Effects that happen when cards are purged are resolved, then the Glory of the purged cards is added to your **purged Glory**.

---

## Undo

The **↩︎ button** undoes your last action, as often as you like, except actions that revealed new cards (starting a turn, progressing, drawing). Undo is not available during the tutorial.

---

## Scoring and Saving

Your game is **saved automatically** on your device after each action, and synced with your account when you are signed in. At the end of the game, your score is the total **Glory** ({{glory}}) of every card in your kingdom (deck, discard pile, play and permanent cards) plus your purged Glory. With an account, your scores appear on the leaderboard.
