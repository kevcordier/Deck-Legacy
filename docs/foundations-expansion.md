# Feudal Kingdom: Foundations

Working notes for the Foundations expansion (cards 0–156, one logo). Source screens: `../kl-cards/FeudalKingdomFoundations/<n>.png`; stickers: sticker sheet v4.1, page 2 (Nobles, Mechanics, Romance, Church, Science, Artisans, Black Plague).

## Structure

- 3 campaigns of 6 rounds: The Black Plague (cards 2–15), Invasion (16–29), Nature's Revenge (30–44). A kingdom plays each campaign once.
- 6 foundations: Science (45–63), Church, Romance, Artisans, Nobles, Mechanics (64–156). A kingdom plays each foundation once; only the stickers of its foundations are usable.
- Each campaign card has a round track; at the green boxes (rounds 1 and 4) the player discovers one foundation. **Decision:** the player picks, at each green box, a foundation not yet played in this kingdom (no choice at campaign start).
- A foundation cover card discovers some of its cards and adds the others "to the back of the box" (undiscovered cards, reachable only by effects).
- Science and herbs are shown in the UI only when the kingdom has the foundation that uses them.

## Second edition rules used by this expansion

Summary of the Kingdom Legacy 2nd edition rule sheet, limited to what Foundations needs.

- **Rule of nine** (golden rule 5): no resource sticker on a card that already produces 9 or more. Production with a science icon still counts as production.
- **New resources** (science, herbs): "any 1 resource" includes them; "add any 1 resource sticker" may add them even when the effect names stickers 1–6.
- **Undiscover**: reset the card and put it back into the box in serial-number order (if the undiscovered order was rearranged, insert it before the first card with a higher number).
- **Choose a side**: a card with red arrows at the top: when discovered, the player chooses which of its 2 sides is face up, for good.
- **Equipment**: a third, red banner "Equip <keyword>".
  - Not equipped: no production and no effects, but it is a card in play with its keywords and fame, and can be upgraded.
  - Equipping is an action (never a reaction, e.g. not during a "when played" effect): place it under a card in play with the required keyword.
  - Equipped: no longer a card of its own; its production, fame, keywords and effects count as written on the holder.
  - Holder blocked or destroyed → its equipment too. Holder changes orientation, is discarded or leaves play → its equipment is discarded.
  - Crossing out the holder's production crosses out the equipment's too.
  - An equipped card cannot be upgraded, un-equipped or re-equipped.
- **Tabbing**: a tab sticker sticks out of the card, so a tabbed card is visible wherever it is in the deck.
- **Checkbox ⊗**: mark an unmarked box; if it has an icon, do it (a resource icon gains it). Boxes are marked in any order unless the card says otherwise. Marking a box on another card triggers the same effects as if that card marked it.
- **Defeating enemies**: only cards with a defeat instruction can be defeated (destroy, turn or mark a box).
- **Blocking**: a blocked card does not exist. When the blocking card is discarded at end of turn or round, the blocked card is discarded too; discarded during the turn, the blocked card is released (not played) into the play area.
- **Effect scope**: by default, cards in play (not permanents). "Kingdom" = deck, play, discard and permanents, not blocked cards.
- **Effect timing**: simultaneous effects are all resolved, in the player's order, even if their card is discarded meanwhile. Cards played together are all in play when "when played" effects resolve.
