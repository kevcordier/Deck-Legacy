## Answers (user, 2026-10-19)

- Undiscovered cards ("back of the box") are the discoverable pile.
- Black Plague: only a person discarded **from play** marks its infection sticker, a card blocked by a card in play (Quarantine) included; not from the top of the deck.
- Quarantine blocks any person in play, infected or not: choosing who to block is up to the player.
- Cursed Lands: the 3 random lands come from the whole kingdom (deck, discard, play) and leave the deck until the card is upgraded; "any 20 resources" = 20 resources in total, of any types.
- Haunted Souls: at the start of the round, after the shuffle, 3 random persons of the deck are blocked.
- Gray Mines and Bear Cub: 0 glory, except −5 when purged.
- Only 2 foundations are discovered per campaign (6 for 3 campaigns), so the choice at a green box is never empty.
- Card 28 is a copy of 25; Fancy Wagon is card 152; Aleta's fame is the sum of her medals not crossed out; Mosaics costs N of each; Golden Ring's "any" is chosen each time; Stone Roads is unlimited; Gregorius' ⌛ tasks are marked by ending the turn; Mystic Scholar upgrades by discarding 5 cards with production.

## Answers (user, 2026-10-20) — Church

- Archbishops: "a new Archbishop" = different from the previous round's (an earlier one may come back).
- Archbishops: choosing one = choosing a card **and** its side; that card blocks the 2 other Archbishop cards for the round.
- "Blessed cards have +X / Y production": each production of a blessed card gets +X or +Y (chosen when producing).
- A blocked Archbishop keeps its marked tasks and its used one-time blessing; it just has no effect that round.

## Answers (user, 2026-10-20) — Romance

- A card with the Knight sticker (11) is a knight everywhere, base game included: sticker tags count in selectors.
- Famous Court: write the person's current fame (stickers included); each name only once (two copies of a card share a name).
- Andoniel: ladies (printed scarf) and knights with a scarf sticker may stay in play.
- Guinevere: knights' whole production is doubled (printed, stickers, bonuses).

## Answers (user, 2026-10-20) — Artisans

- Jeweler: every iron gain (production and effects) may become goods while the Jeweler is in play.
- Statues: medals are written in order (left to right, top to bottom), spending 2–20 stone (value 1–10).
